# PROJECT DELIVERY PLAN

## PNP-ITMS Personnel and Assignment Information System (PAIS 2.0): 10-Week Implementation Timeline

**July 20–September 27, 2026**

## Delivery Schedule

### Week 1 | July 20–26, 2026

**Focus: Project Planning, Requirements, and System Design**

- Confirm the project scope, target users, functional requirements, user roles, acceptance criteria, and required reports and documents.
- Design the application workflow, shared personnel database, module relationships, REST API structure, navigation, and responsive interface.
- Deliver the approved implementation plan, UI/UX plan, SDLC plan, initial data model, and development environment.

### Week 2 | July 27–August 2, 2026

**Focus: Core Personnel Information and Management**

- Develop the central personnel profile with personal, contact, employment, assignment, rank, status, and identification details.
- Implement authorized personnel create, view, update, delete, search, filtering, validation, and database synchronization.
- Deliver a working Personnel Information Module and Management Center that serve as the source for all linked records.

### Week 3 | August 3–9, 2026

**Focus: Assignments, Orders, Awards, and Initial Leave Workflow**

- Implement assignment histories, official orders, awards, and the initial leave-record workflow using unique personnel identifiers.
- Add personnel selection, editable document templates, automatic profile linking, validation, and authorized record maintenance.
- Deliver connected assignment, order, award, and leave records with print-ready document preparation.

### Week 4 | August 10–16, 2026

**Focus: Education and Training Records**

- Develop education histories covering schools, degrees, education levels, attendance dates, and supporting details.
- Develop training histories covering courses, providers, hours, dates, certificates, editing, validation, and bulk data handling.
- Deliver searchable, exportable Education and Training Modules linked to the correct personnel profiles.

### Week 5 | August 17–23, 2026

**Focus: Promotions and Time-in-Grade Automation**

- Implement promotion histories, previous and current ranks, effective dates, editing, deletion, and personnel-rank synchronization.
- Add automatic time-in-grade and promotion-eligibility calculations with missing, invalid, future, leap-year, and boundary-date handling.
- Deliver a verified Promotion Module with accurate histories, calculations, validation messages, and profile integration.

### Week 6 | August 24–30, 2026

**Focus: Leave Management and Calendar**

- Complete leave applications with leave type, inclusive dates, reason, approval status, approver, remarks, editing, and deletion.
- Implement a leave calendar with personnel names, multi-day schedules, status indicators, event details, and record updates.
- Deliver an integrated Leave Module and calendar that support administrative review and schedule monitoring.

### Week 7 | August 31–September 6, 2026

**Focus: Reports, Search, and Document Outputs**

- Develop Alpha List, Current Assignments, Current Leave, Education, Training, Promotion, Orders, and Awards reports.
- Add report search, filters, print layouts, CSV export, direct PDF export, clear filenames, and consistent result formatting.
- Deliver accurate report and document outputs that match the selected data, personnel links, and applied filters.

### Week 8 | September 7–13, 2026

**Focus: Integration, Security, and Technical Verification**

- Integrate all modules, shared personnel data, editable templates, automatic linked records, printing, PDF export, and responsive navigation.
- Complete Supabase Auth, signed sessions, superadmin/admin/view-only access, protected API mutations, administrator accounts, and health checks.
- Deliver a production build supported by automated tests, Supabase CRUD verification, defect corrections, and an updated deployment checklist.

### Week 9 | September 14–20, 2026

**Focus: User Acceptance and Production Readiness**

- Conduct end-to-end, accessibility, print, PDF, security, and user-acceptance testing with authorized stakeholders and production-like data.
- Resolve approved defects, validate data migration and Row Level Security, verify backup restoration, and finalize acceptance evidence.
- Deliver the signed UAT record, resolved-defect log, security/privacy review, deployment approval, and rollback plan.

### Week 10 | September 21–27, 2026

**Focus: Production Deployment, Training, and Handover**

- Configure production credentials, HTTPS, database policies, backups, monitoring, authorized accounts, and the approved hosting environment.
- Deploy the system, verify critical workflows and record counts, orient users and administrators, and provide operating documentation.
- Deliver the live system, deployment and smoke-test records, user handover package, support contacts, and post-deployment monitoring report.

## Completion Criteria

The project is complete when all ten weekly deliverables are accepted, the production system passes its critical-workflow smoke tests, authorized stakeholders sign the user-acceptance record, backup restoration is verified, and ownership is formally transferred for ongoing monitoring and maintenance.
