# Brag Plan: PAIS 2.0 (PNP-ITMS Personnel and Assignment Information System)

## What is this app?
PAIS 2.0 is the official institutional personnel management, promotion processing, assignment tracking, and database-backed Excel worksheet generation platform for the Philippine National Police Information Technology Management Service (PNP-ITMS).

## The angle
One connected enterprise repository replaces fragmented paper workflows. Canonical personnel records synchronize directly across assignment orders, education records, promotion rosters, and official single-sheet PNP promotional examination worksheets without data drift or duplicate entry.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Cinematic institutional 3D insignia reveal of PNP-ITMS PAIS 2.0 surrounded by encrypted signal routes and telemetry, establishing a sovereign, secure enterprise platform.

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **Automated Strength & Analytics Dashboard:** Real-time PCO, PNCO, and NUP counts with duty status, active assignments, and promotion review indicators.
2. **Promotional Qualification Engine:** Time in Grade (TIG) and Time in Position (TIP) evaluation with rank filtering and pre-worksheet record validation.
3. **Database-Backed Promotional Worksheet Generation:** High-fidelity 3D motion mapping records from database fields directly into the official 17-column PNP-ITMS landscape worksheet template.
4. **Interactive Excel Integration Center:** Full-fledged in-browser spreadsheet module with Excel ribbon, formula calculation parity, and instant single-sheet `.xlsx` download.

## Outro / punchline
"CONNECTED RECORDS. CONSISTENT WORKSHEETS. ONE SYSTEM." Floating multi-panel 3D showcase of all connected modules resolving cleanly into the PAIS 2.0 crest.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry & Exploration:** Authenticate into PAIS 2.0 → Inspect automated headcount and active assignments on Dashboard.
2. **Promotion & Candidate Selection:** Open Promotional Module → Select "Police Master Sergeant (PMSg)" → Review candidate roster → Select exactly 5 candidates → Validate required records.
3. **Worksheet Generation & Output:** Open Excel Worksheets center → Trigger dynamic title resolution → Map personnel database records to 17 official worksheet columns → Review landscape print-ready grid → Export official `.xlsx`.
