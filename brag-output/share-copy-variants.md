I am proud to showcase PAIS 2.0 (Personnel and Assignment Information System), an institutional enterprise platform engineered for the Philippine National Police Information Technology Management Service (PNP-ITMS).

PAIS 2.0 establishes a single canonical source of truth for police personnel records, automating Time in Grade (TIG) promotional qualifications, duty assignments, and generating print-ready, single-sheet official Excel worksheets with full formula parity.

Key Capabilities:
• Real-time automated strength analytics across PCO, PNCO, and NUP categories
• Interactive Excel Worksheet Center with full ribbon controls and live database sync
• Pre-export record validation ensuring 100% data integrity on promotional examination documents
• Unified management of Special Orders, Leave Calendars, Education, and Official Documents
